SHOW THE SONG

To show what Pandora, Spotify or any app is playing (and play, pause and skip it), Android needs to give ByteFall Viz notification access. It reads no notifications, only what's playing.

Switch greyed out? Android holds it back for apps installed outside a store: open App info, tap ⋮ top right, then Allow restricted settings, and try ALLOW again.

RULES

Encrypted bits fall into the terminal. Drop each one into a column.

A bit decrypts when its number matches the length of the unbroken line it sits in, across or down. Decrypting can bring more bits down into new matches for a chain.

A decrypted bit scores 10 plus its number (a [4] is 14), multiplied by the chain. Blocks wiped out by exploits score a flat 10.

Encryption layers [=] rise from below every 8 drops. Decrypt a bit beside one to peel a layer off; the second peel ([-]) reveals the bit hidden underneath.

Keep your columns below the ======== line. If anything is still above it once the decrypting stops, the trace completes and you're caught.

On Easy and Normal, every 4 bits a single drop decrypts make a NIBBLE, worth 16 bonus points.

Easy shows the next bit, and a drop that clears nothing only takes one bar off the CHAIN METER (on Normal it empties it).

Hard is a full byte: an 8×8 terminal with bits up to 8, and new layers come faster as your score climbs. Every 8 bits a single drop decrypts make a BYTE, worth 256 bonus points, and 4 more a NIBBLE, worth 16. Its CHAIN METER empties after every chain: the 5 links have to come in one.

Tap a column or its button, or press 1–7, to drop

RECORDS

EXPLOITS

Fill the CHAIN METER (5 links, carried over from drop to drop) to get a random exploit from your equipped slots. It waits in the exploit button (under the grid): tap it to arm the exploit as your next drop, then drop it like a bit and it runs wherever it lands. Once armed, there's no taking it back.

§ WORM VIRUS

Burrows down the column it lands in and wipes out every block there.

+ BUFFER OVERFLOW

Floods memory: every bit goes up by 1. A bit pushed past 7 gets re-encrypted under two layers.

◈ TROJAN

Slips in quietly, then wipes out every block touching the spot where it lands.

? RNG

Scrambles every bit to a new random number.

BITFLIP

Turns every column upside down.

# DICTIONARY ATTACK

Throws every known key at the encryption: every layer on the board loses one level at once.

@ KEYLOGGER

Records the incoming stream: shows your next 3 bits for the next 10 drops.

⇆ PIVOT

Moves sideways through the network: the column it lands in swaps places with the one beside it. Pick the column, then tap the neighbor (← or →) to swap with: once picked, you're committed. Edge columns swap with their only neighbor.

SWAP

Spoofs two addresses: any two bits on the board trade places. Armed, tap a bit on the grid, then another (tap the first again to put it back); they swap, and any match they make decrypts.

~ PACKET SNIFFER

Taps the incoming stream: for your next 3 bits, tap CURRENT (or press ↑ / ↓) to choose each one's number.

! LOGIC BOMB

Lands as a block counting down from 3. Three drops later it detonates, wiping out everything in the 5×5 around it.

◎ HONEYPOT

Lands as a trap. When a bit next to it decrypts, every bit with that number within 2 cells of the trap decrypts too.

_ BACKDOOR

Opens a way in underneath: the entire bottom row is deleted, layers included, and everything drops by one.

* RAINBOW TABLE

Looks up the most common number on the board and decrypts every bit showing it.

BLACK BOX

Nobody knows what's inside. Armed, it opens into a random exploit: any of them, equipped or not.

DAILY DROP FREE

Once a day: a free exploit for your next game (not in DAILY, PUZZLE or VS), 25 KEYS, and 3 each of BUGS, CACHE and CRYPTO. Every 7th day in a row you play, 3 MASTER KEYS too.

BOOSTERS

Bought with KEYS, earned by playing: 1 for every 10 bits decrypted, more for chains of 5 and 7 links, first puzzle solves, achievements, levels and the day's first daily game. Switch them on from the main menu; each is used up in the game it's on in (SECOND CHANCE only if it saves you). Not in DAILY or VS.

================================================================================

STARTER EXPLOITS

An exploit of your own to take into a game: up to 2 (tap a STARTER slot on the main menu to pick one), one each side of the exploit button. Tap one there to arm it as your next drop, as one the CHAIN METER earned; each is used once, and the ones you don't use stay yours. Each costs KEYS and RESOURCES, earned by how you decrypt: BUGS down a column, CACHE across a row, CRYPTO for chain links from the 3rd on, ROOTKITS for a bit decrypted across and down at once, layers broken and BYTES. Short of the price? A MASTER KEY (every 5th level, the day's first daily game, the day's first VS win) buys any exploit. A side taken in empty, or once its starter is used, is the BLACK MARKET: an exploit or BLACK BOX at these prices, a different one every 4 drops. It opens once the first encryption layer rises (in ZEN, after 8 drops). In CLASSIC, BLITZ and ZEN.

================================================================================

BLACK BOXES

A random pull, cheaper than what it's likely to hold. It waits sealed in its slot; tap it in the game and it rolls, landing on an exploit (any of its tier, locked or not) to arm when you like, or now and then an ANTI-EXPLOIT that goes off at once: ADWARE (a pop-up covers a drop button for 3 drops), SPYWARE (your next 3 bits are hidden) or RANSOMWARE (3 bits locked under a layer). The higher the box, the better the odds.

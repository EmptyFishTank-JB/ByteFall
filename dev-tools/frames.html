Draw the bots' animations a frame at a time, on their own pixel grid (each square is one pixel of a bot; the dashed box is the bot's 16x17). UNDO and REDO (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover everything: painting, and adding, deleting, moving and timing frames. Your animations are kept in this browser; EXPORT saves one as a file to hand over to be built into the game.

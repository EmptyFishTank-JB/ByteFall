ByteFall Privacy Policy

Developer: EmptyFishTank Studio • Effective Date: October 3, 2026

TL;DR Summary: ByteFall does not collect, transmit, track, or sell any of your personal information. All game data (such as high scores, Hashes, unlocks, and settings) is stored strictly locally on your own device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how EmptyFishTank Studio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you play the ByteFall mobile application and web game (the "Application"). We respect your privacy and have designed ByteFall to operate as a self-contained offline experience.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). When you install and use ByteFall:

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
  • We do not access your device contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not use third-party analytics trackers or advertising SDKs inside the Application.

3. Local Device Storage

ByteFall uses your device's built-in local storage (such as Android application storage or browser localStorage) solely to save your gameplay progress. This includes:

  • High scores, game statistics, and earned Hashes.
  • Unlocked achievements, upgrades, and cosmetic terminal preferences.
  • Audio, haptic, and gameplay configuration settings.

This data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us or any external servers. You can clear or delete this local data at any time by clearing the Application's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the Application.

4. Third-Party Platform Distribution

If you download ByteFall through the Google Play Store or access the web version via GitHub Pages, those platform providers (Google LLC or GitHub, Inc.) may collect standard platform-level telemetry, crash reports, or download metrics governed by their own respective privacy policies. EmptyFishTank Studio does not receive or store personally identifiable information from these platforms.

5. Children's Privacy

Because ByteFall does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user, the Application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child data protection regulations. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please reach out to us at the email address below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update ByteFall in the future with features that alter how data is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
